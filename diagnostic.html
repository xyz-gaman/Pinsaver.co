Questions

Why does Save sometimes open the file in a new tab?

Pinterest doesn't let other websites read its video files directly, so some browsers can't save them from inside the page. When that happens Pinsave opens the file and tells you the one extra tap: on the player, tap the three dots and choose Download, or long-press and choose Download.

I only got a poster image for a video pin

Pinterest occasionally returns only the first frame for a pin. Tap Retry, or try again in a minute. If it keeps happening, the pin may be private or removed.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free, and no signup. Nothing is installed.

Where do my links go?

Your browser asks Pinterest directly for the pin's details. For short links (pin.it) Pinsave may use a small helper server, or public helper services, to find the pin number. Don't paste links you consider private.

Can I use what I download?

Pins belong to their creators. Download for personal use, and ask permission or give credit before reposting or using anything commercially.
